## We're here

Digital Ascend Arts Production was founded with a simple belief: **theatre belongs everywhere**. Not just in velvet-seated auditoriums, but on the screens you carry in your pocket and the ones on your wall at home.

Our debut one-act play, *The Last Row*, is in development. It asks what happens when the distance between performer and audience collapses — a question we're not just exploring on stage, but in how we release our work.

### What to expect

- Original 90-minute plays written specifically for both stage and streaming
- Behind-the-scenes essays on this blog
- Tickets for local performances
- Recorded productions available to stream

Check back soon. The house lights are dimming.
