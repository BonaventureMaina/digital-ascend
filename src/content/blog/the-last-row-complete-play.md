*The Last Row* is a 90‑minute one‑act play by Digital Ascend Arts Production. It tells the story of Martin Vale, a theatre critic who has spent thirty years in the back row — and the night everything changes.

When Martin's debut script is accepted by a small fringe theatre, he arrives on opening night to find the entire cast has walked out. With one understudy, a stage manager who won't flatter him, and an audience already in their seats, he is forced to perform his own words for the first time.

What follows is a collision of critic and creation, a play within a play, and a man discovering that the distance between spectator and performer is thinner than he thought.
