## The fourth wall is a screen now

For centuries, theatre has been defined by presence — you had to be in the room. But the room has changed. It's now a phone on a bus, a laptop in a kitchen, a projector in a living room.

That's not a threat to theatre. It's an invitation.

### What streaming changes

- **Geography stops being a barrier.** A play performed in one city can reach an audience on the other side of the world.
- **Time stops being a barrier.** Recorded productions let people watch when they can, not just when we perform.
- **Intimacy changes shape.** A close-up on an actor's face during a vulnerable monologue is something no back-row seat can replicate.

### What it doesn't change

- The words still matter.
- The performance still matters.
- The audience still matters.

We're not replacing the stage. We're building a second one.
